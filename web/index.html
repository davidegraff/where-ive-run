How do I get my Strava archive?
  1. On strava.com, go to Settings → My Account → Download or Delete Your Account → Get Started.
  2. Under “Download Request”, click Request Your Archive.
  3. Strava emails you a link to a zip file, usually within a few hours. Download it and drop it above. There's no need to unzip it.
🔒 Your files are uploaded to this app's server, read there, and deleted once they've been imported. The activities found in them are kept on the server so the map is still here next time; Delete all removes them. City names are worked out on the server from a list of towns, so your locations aren't sent anywhere else.
